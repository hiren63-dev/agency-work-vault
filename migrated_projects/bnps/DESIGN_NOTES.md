# BNPS experience decisions

The site helps finance leaders, business owners, developers, trustees and individuals establish whether the firm handles their matter, which professional works in that area, how to reach the firm and what an engagement involves.

## Current visual direction

The homepage hero contains no people or photography. Three abstract planes represent the connected audit, tax and advisory disciplines. Pointer movement changes the perspective; accessible buttons recompose the planes and update the practice link. A soft background field provides restrained ambient motion. The duplicated navigation strip and repeated navigation panels below the hero have been removed.

Five named constituent firms connect to BNPS in a scroll-driven merger diagram. Paths fill as the reader scrolls, and individual sources respond to hover and keyboard focus. This is a relationship diagram, not a dated chronology. On mobile, sources stack above the destination. Reduced-motion mode shows the complete diagram without pinning.

The firm page contains the formation diagram, a five-chapter reading timeline, interactive professional principles, purpose and direction, and contact access. The timeline uses a progressing rail and active nodes. Sector cards use abstract relationship diagrams, context-specific questions and service links. Patterns and highlights respond to hover and focus. The horizontal scroll distance was shortened and controls fit within smaller desktop viewports.

Careers and Insights are explicitly coming soon. Their illustrations depict an unfinished page layout with restrained motion. They do not advertise fictitious vacancies or articles.

All 14 partners remain in the home carousel and directory. Four city filters and an all-city option replace search. Shared partners appear in both relevant cities. Changes animate card positions and entrances, with an immediate reduced-motion fallback.

The contact page routes enquiries to the existing form on bnpsca.com. An on-site delivery service and confirmed recipient remain outstanding. The generated Bengaluru portrait is retained under concepts only; it is not used in the hero.

## References and content review

- BNPS About Us: https://bnpsca.com/about-us and homepage: https://bnpsca.com/ were reviewed alongside the supplied content.md. Useful content brought forward includes the constituent firms, disciplines, people and mentoring, technology, professional education, purpose and principles. Individual qualifications and authored works remain in partner profiles.
- Framer Scroll Step Timeline: https://www.framer.com/marketplace/components/scroll-step-timeline/ and live preview: https://scrollstepstimeline.framer.website/ were inspected in the browser and screenshots taken. The progressing line, active node and halo informed the firm's timeline. The implementation uses original HTML, CSS and JavaScript; paid component code was not extracted.
- Earlier references include https://21st.dev/community/components/explore/marquee-component, https://www.framer.com/marketplace/components/horizontal-parallax/ and https://credibility.stanford.edu/guidelines/index.html.

The factual 1974 constituent-firm date is retained. Conflicting headline age claims, aggregate experience totals, client counts, outcome case studies, testimonials and unverified marketing claims have not been imported. Professional eligibility claims were not expanded into marketing badges.

## Verification

Static checks cover generated pages, links/assets, metadata and JSON-LD. Browser checks cover hero selection, absence of hero portraits and duplicate navigation, merger progress, timeline states, principle tabs, sector movement and visible controls. Mobile pages were checked for horizontal overflow, and reduced-motion behavior was verified. No additional runtime library is required for the new interactions.
